# Privacy Policy (PLACEHOLDER — not legal text)

This placeholder must be replaced with a reviewed policy before any store
submission. It lists the intended data practices so the code can be checked
against them.

- **Audience:** may include children. Designed to follow COPPA, GDPR-K and the
  Apple App Store Kids/Families and Google Play Families policies.
- **Data collected (only when online features are on):** an anonymous
  account ID, a display name and server chosen from preset lists, career
  save data (game state only, plus the previous upload as a backup) and
  ranking summaries. No real name, email, contacts, location, advertising
  ID or free text.
- **Shown to other players:** a preset or filter-approved display name and
  career statistics only. There is no chat.
- **In-game social feed (Kickfie):** fictional, generated on the device from
  preset templates and preset fan names. Nothing is posted or received.
- **Third parties:** Supabase (hosting and database). No ads or analytics SDKs
  in the first version.
- **Deletion:** Online & settings → Delete my account removes the anonymous
  account, profile, online careers and rankings from the server. Careers on
  the device can be deleted at the same time or from the Careers screen.
- **External links:** behind a parental gate.
